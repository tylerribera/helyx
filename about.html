about

A quiet journal
for the protocol you're already running.

Helyx exists because the people taking peptides, stacking supplements, tracking sleep, and tuning recovery don't have a clean place to write any of it down. Notes apps are too messy. Spreadsheets stop being fun after a week. Medical apps weren't built for this.

What Helyx is

A logbook. You write down what you took, when you took it, and how you felt. Helyx organizes the timeline, surfaces patterns, and — if you upgrade to Premium — generates a clinical report you can hand to a doctor.

That's the entire product. No social feed. No store. No supplements to sell you. No affiliate links.

What Helyx is not

Not a medical device. Not a diagnostic tool. Not a replacement for a doctor. We don't give protocol advice and we don't recommend compounds.

If you're running a protocol, Helyx helps you see it clearly. The decisions are yours.

How we think about your data

Your log lives on your device first. Sync is optional, encrypted, and tied to your account — not to an advertising profile. We don't sell data. We don't have ads. The free tier and Premium tier both follow the same rule.

You can export your full history as JSON or PDF at any time. You can delete your account from inside the app and your server-side data goes with it.

Who we are

A small team that got tired of using the Notes app for this. Helyx is independent, self-funded, and not in a hurry. We ship slowly and we don't add features that don't earn their place.
