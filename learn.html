Privacy policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

1. What we collect

Account information (email, password hash). Journal data you choose to sync (entries, check-ins, schedules, notes). Basic usage analytics (which screens you opened, anonymized).

2. What we don't collect

We do not collect contact lists, location history, advertising IDs, or third-party identifiers. We do not run ads.

3. Where your data lives

Your journal lives on your device first. If you turn on cloud sync, encrypted copies are stored on servers in the United States. We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

4. Who we share with

Payment processors (to handle Premium subscriptions). Cloud infrastructure (to host encrypted data). Law enforcement only if compelled by a valid legal order. We never sell your data.

5. Your rights

Export your full history at any time as JSON or PDF. Delete your account from inside the app — server-side data is permanently removed within 30 days. Email hello@helyx.us for anything else.
