Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 6, 2026

Helyx LLC ("Helyx," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at helyx.us (the "Site") or make a purchase. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

When you create an account, place an order, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Identity information: first name, last name
  • Contact information: email address, shipping address
  • Institutional information: laboratory or institution name (optional)
  • Account information: email and encrypted password
  • Order information: items purchased, quantities, order totals

b. Payment Information

Payment is processed by secure third-party payment processors (e.g., NMI, PayRam). We do not store your full credit card number, CVV, or bank account details on our servers. We may receive and store a truncated card number (last 4 digits) and transaction reference IDs for record-keeping.

c. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information: browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent on pages, click behavior
  • Network information: IP address, approximate geographic location
  • Referral data: the website or source that directed you to our Site

d. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember your cart contents
  • Analyze site traffic and usage patterns
  • Serve relevant advertising via Meta (Facebook) Pixel

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill orders — including shipping, payment processing, and order confirmations
  • Manage your account — registration, authentication, and password resets
  • Communicate with you — order updates, shipping notifications, and responses to inquiries
  • Improve our Site — analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Prevent fraud — detect and prevent fraudulent transactions or unauthorized access
  • Comply with legal obligations — respond to lawful requests from authorities and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Marketing — send promotional emails (only with your consent; you may opt out at any time)

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only with:

  • Payment processors (NMI, PayRam) — to process transactions securely
  • Shipping carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx) — to deliver your orders
  • Email service providers (Resend) — to send transactional and marketing emails
  • Analytics providers (Meta/Facebook Pixel, Google) — to analyze site traffic and advertising performance
  • Law enforcement or regulatory bodies — when required by law, subpoena, or to protect our legal rights

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Maintain your account and provide services
  • Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption on all data in transit
  • Encrypted password storage (bcrypt hashing)
  • PCI-compliant payment processing via third-party tokenization
  • Access controls limiting employee access to personal data

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out of marketing: Unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time via the link in each email
  • Cookie preferences: Adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@helyx.us. We will respond within 30 days.

7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

To submit a CCPA request, email us at support@helyx.us with the subject line "CCPA Request."
